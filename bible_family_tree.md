
# FAMILY TREE

# Adam
Sons: Seth
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Adam was the first man in the biblical account. He had other sons and daughters whose names are not recorded here.
Verses: Genesis 4:1-2, Genesis 4:25, Genesis 5:1-5

# Eve
Sons: Cain, Abel, Seth
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Eve was Adam's wife and the mother of Cain, Abel, and Seth.
Verses: Genesis 4:1-2, Genesis 4:25

# Cain
Parents: Adam, Eve
Sons: Enoch
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Cain was Adam and Eve's first named son. He had a son named Enoch.
Verses: Genesis 4:1, Genesis 4:17

# Abel
Parents: Adam, Eve
Sons: Unknown
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Abel was a son of Adam and Eve. The Bible does not name any children of Abel.
Verses: Genesis 4:2-8

# Seth
Parents: Adam, Eve
Sons: Enosh
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Seth was born after Abel's death. Adam was 130 years old when Seth was born.
Verses: Genesis 4:25, Genesis 5:3-8

# Enoch
Parents: Cain
Sons: Unknown
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: This Enoch was the son of Cain. He is different from Enoch in Seth's family line.
Verses: Genesis 4:17

# Enosh
Parents: Seth
Sons: Kenan
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Enosh was the son of Seth.
Verses: Genesis 4:26, Genesis 5:6-11

# Kenan
Parents: Enosh
Sons: Mahalalel
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Kenan was the son of Enosh.
Verses: Genesis 5:9-14

# Mahalalel
Parents: Kenan
Sons: Jared
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Mahalalel was the son of Kenan.
Verses: Genesis 5:12-17

# Jared
Parents: Mahalalel
Sons: Enoch
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Jared was the father of Enoch in Seth's family line.
Verses: Genesis 5:15-20

# Enoch
Parents: Jared
Sons: Methuselah
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: This Enoch walked with God and was taken by God. He was the son of Jared and the father of Methuselah.
Verses: Genesis 5:18-24

# Methuselah
Parents: Enoch
Sons: Lamech
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Methuselah was the son of Enoch and the father of Lamech.
Verses: Genesis 5:21-27

# Lamech
Parents: Methuselah
Sons: Noah
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Lamech was the father of Noah.
Verses: Genesis 5:25-31

# Noah
Parents: Lamech
Sons: Shem, Ham, Japheth
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Noah had three named sons: Shem, Ham, and Japheth.
Verses: Genesis 5:28-32, Genesis 6:9-10

# Shem
Parents: Noah
Sons: Arpachshad
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Shem was one of Noah's three sons. The genealogy in Genesis follows his family line toward Abraham.
Verses: Genesis 5:32, Genesis 10:21-22, Genesis 11:10-11

# Ham
Parents: Noah
Sons: Cush, Mizraim, Put, Canaan
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Ham was one of Noah's three sons.
Verses: Genesis 9:18, Genesis 10:1, Genesis 10:6

# Japheth
Parents: Noah
Sons: Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech, Tiras
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Japheth was one of Noah's three sons.
Verses: Genesis 9:18, Genesis 10:1-2

# Arpachshad
Parents: Shem
Sons: Shelah
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Arpachshad was the son of Shem and an ancestor of Abraham.
Verses: Genesis 10:22-24, Genesis 11:10-13

# Shelah
Parents: Arpachshad
Sons: Eber
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Shelah was the son of Arpachshad and the father of Eber.
Verses: Genesis 11:12-15

# Eber
Parents: Shelah
Sons: Peleg, Joktan
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Eber was the father of Peleg and Joktan. The line leading to Abraham continues through Peleg.
Verses: Genesis 10:25, Genesis 11:14-17

# Peleg
Parents: Eber
Sons: Reu
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Peleg was the son of Eber and the father of Reu.
Verses: Genesis 10:25, Genesis 11:16-19

# Reu
Parents: Peleg
Sons: Serug
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Reu was the son of Peleg and the father of Serug.
Verses: Genesis 11:18-21

# Serug
Parents: Reu
Sons: Nahor
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Serug was the son of Reu and the father of Nahor.
Verses: Genesis 11:20-23

# Nahor
Parents: Serug
Sons: Terah
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: This Nahor was the father of Terah and the grandfather of Abram, Nahor, and Haran.
Verses: Genesis 11:22-26

# Terah
Parents: Nahor
Sons: Abram, Nahor, Haran
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Terah was the father of Abram, Nahor, and Haran.
Verses: Genesis 11:24-32

# Abram
Parents: Terah
Sons: Ishmael, Isaac
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Abram was later renamed Abraham. His sons named in the biblical account include Ishmael and Isaac. Abraham also had other sons with Keturah.
Verses: Genesis 11:26-32, Genesis 16:15-16, Genesis 17:5, Genesis 21:1-5, Genesis 25:1-6

# Nahor
Parents: Terah
Sons: Unknown
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: This Nahor was Abram's brother and the son of Terah. He is different from Nahor, the father of Terah.
Verses: Genesis 11:26-29

# Haran
Parents: Terah
Sons: Lot, Milcah, Iscah
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Haran was a son of Terah and the father of Lot, Milcah, and Iscah.
Verses: Genesis 11:27-29

# Lot
Parents: Haran
Sons: Unknown
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Lot was the son of Haran and the nephew of Abram. His daughters are mentioned, but their names are not given in the account.
Verses: Genesis 11:27-31, Genesis 19:30-38

# Milcah
Parents: Haran
Sons: Unknown
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Milcah was the daughter of Haran and later became the wife of Nahor, Abram's brother.
Verses: Genesis 11:27-29

# Iscah
Parents: Haran
Sons: Unknown
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Iscah was named as a daughter of Haran.
Verses: Genesis 11:29

# Sarah
Parents: Terah
Sons: Isaac
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Sarah was Abraham's wife. Her relationship to Terah is inferred from Abraham's statement that she was his father's daughter but not his mother's daughter. Isaac was Sarah's son.
Verses: Genesis 17:15-19, Genesis 20:12, Genesis 21:1-3
