
# FAMILY TREE

# Adam
Sons: Cain, Abel, Seth
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Adam was the first man in the biblical account. He had other sons and daughters whose names are not recorded here.
Verses: Genesis 4:1-2, Genesis 4:25, Genesis 5:1-5

# Eve
Parents: Unknown
Spouse: Adam
Sons: Cain, Abel, Seth
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Eve was Adam’s wife and the mother of Cain, Abel, and Seth.
Verses: Genesis 4:1-2, Genesis 4:25

# Cain
Parents: Adam, Eve
Sons: Enoch (son of Cain)
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Cain was Adam and Eve’s first named son. He had a son named Enoch.
Verses: Genesis 4:1, Genesis 4:17

# Abel
Parents: Adam, Eve
Sons: Unknown
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Abel was a son of Adam and Eve. The Bible does not name any children of Abel.
Verses: Genesis 4:2-8

# Seth
Parents: Adam, Eve
Sons: Enosh
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Seth was born after Abel’s death. Adam was 130 years old when Seth was born.
Verses: Genesis 4:25, Genesis 5:3-8

# Enoch (son of Cain)
Parents: Cain
Sons: Unknown
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: This Enoch was the son of Cain. He is different from Enoch, the son of Jared, in Seth’s family line.
Verses: Genesis 4:17

# Enosh
Parents: Seth
Sons: Kenan
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Enosh was the son of Seth.
Verses: Genesis 4:26, Genesis 5:6-11

# Kenan
Parents: Enosh
Sons: Mahalalel
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Kenan was the son of Enosh.
Verses: Genesis 5:9-14

# Mahalalel
Parents: Kenan
Sons: Jared
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Mahalalel was the son of Kenan.
Verses: Genesis 5:12-17

# Jared
Parents: Mahalalel
Sons: Enoch (son of Jared)
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Jared was the father of Enoch in Seth’s family line.
Verses: Genesis 5:15-20

# Enoch (son of Jared)
Parents: Jared
Sons: Methuselah
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: This Enoch walked with God and was taken by God. He was the son of Jared and the father of Methuselah. He is different from Enoch, the son of Cain.
Verses: Genesis 5:18-24

# Methuselah
Parents: Enoch (son of Jared)
Sons: Lamech
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Methuselah was the son of Enoch and the father of Lamech.
Verses: Genesis 5:21-27

# Lamech
Parents: Methuselah
Sons: Noah
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Lamech was the father of Noah.
Verses: Genesis 5:25-31

# Noah
Parents: Lamech
Sons: Shem, Ham, Japheth
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Noah had three named sons: Shem, Ham, and Japheth.
Verses: Genesis 5:28-32, Genesis 6:9-10

# Shem
Parents: Noah
Sons: Arpachshad
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Shem was one of Noah’s three sons. The genealogy in Genesis follows his family line toward Abraham.
Verses: Genesis 5:32, Genesis 10:21-22, Genesis 11:10-11

# Ham
Parents: Noah
Sons: Cush, Mizraim, Put, Canaan
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Ham was one of Noah’s three sons.
Verses: Genesis 9:18, Genesis 10:1, Genesis 10:6

# Japheth
Parents: Noah
Sons: Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech, Tiras
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Japheth was one of Noah’s three sons.
Verses: Genesis 9:18, Genesis 10:1-2

# Arpachshad
Parents: Shem
Sons: Shelah
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Arpachshad was the son of Shem and an ancestor of Abraham.
Verses: Genesis 10:22-24, Genesis 11:10-13

# Shelah
Parents: Arpachshad
Sons: Eber
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Shelah was the son of Arpachshad and the father of Eber.
Verses: Genesis 11:12-15

# Eber
Parents: Shelah
Sons: Peleg, Joktan
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Eber was the father of Peleg and Joktan. The line leading to Abraham continues through Peleg.
Verses: Genesis 10:21-25, Genesis 11:14-17

# Peleg
Parents: Eber
Sons: Reu
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Peleg was the son of Eber. The biblical account says the earth was divided in his days.
Verses: Genesis 10:25, Genesis 11:16-19

# Reu
Parents: Peleg
Sons: Serug
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Reu was the son of Peleg and the father of Serug.
Verses: Genesis 11:18-21

# Serug
Parents: Reu
Sons: Nahor (father of Terah)
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Serug was the father of Nahor and an ancestor of Abraham.
Verses: Genesis 11:20-23

# Nahor (father of Terah)
Parents: Serug
Sons: Terah
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: This Nahor was the son of Serug and the father of Terah.
Verses: Genesis 11:22-25

# Terah
Parents: Nahor (father of Terah)
Sons: Abram, Nahor (son of Terah), Haran
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Terah was the father of Abram, Nahor, and Haran.
Verses: Genesis 11:24-32

# Abram
Parents: Terah
Spouse: Sarai
Sons: Ishmael, Isaac
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Abram was later renamed Abraham. He became the father of Ishmael with Hagar and the father of Isaac with Sarah.
Verses: Genesis 11:26-29, Genesis 16:15-16, Genesis 17:5, Genesis 21:1-5

# Nahor (son of Terah)
Parents: Terah
Spouse: Milcah
Sons: Unknown
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: This Nahor was the son of Terah and the brother of Abram. He married Milcah.
Verses: Genesis 11:26-29

# Haran
Parents: Terah
Sons: Lot
Daughters: Milcah, Iscah
Summary: Haran was the son of Terah and the father of Lot, Milcah, and Iscah.
Verses: Genesis 11:26-29

# Lot
Parents: Haran
Sons: Unknown
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Lot was the son of Haran and the nephew of Abram. He traveled with Abram from his homeland.
Verses: Genesis 11:27-31, Genesis 12:4-5

# Milcah
Parents: Haran
Spouse: Nahor (son of Terah)
Sons: Unknown
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Milcah was the daughter of Haran and the wife of Nahor, Abram’s brother.
Verses: Genesis 11:27-29

# Iscah
Parents: Haran
Sons: Unknown
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Iscah was named as a daughter of Haran. The biblical account does not give further details about her.
Verses: Genesis 11:29

# Sarai
Parents: Unknown
Spouse: Abram
Sons: Isaac
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Sarai was Abram’s wife. God later changed her name to Sarah. She gave birth to Isaac in her old age.
Verses: Genesis 11:29-31, Genesis 17:15-19, Genesis 21:1-7

# Hagar
Parents: Unknown
Spouse: Abram
Sons: Ishmael
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Hagar was Sarah’s Egyptian servant and the mother of Ishmael by Abram.
Verses: Genesis 16:1-16, Genesis 21:9-21

# Ishmael
Parents: Abram, Hagar
Sons: Nebaioth, Kedar, Adbeel, Mibsam, Mishma, Dumah, Massa, Hadad, Tema, Jetur, Naphish, Kedemah
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Ishmael was the son of Abram and Hagar. He became the father of twelve princes.
Verses: Genesis 16:15-16, Genesis 17:20, Genesis 25:12-18

# Isaac
Parents: Abram, Sarai
Spouse: Rebekah
Sons: Esau, Jacob
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Isaac was the son promised to Abraham and Sarah. He married Rebekah and became the father of Esau and Jacob.
Verses: Genesis 17:19, Genesis 21:1-7, Genesis 25:19-26

# Rebekah
Parents: Bethuel
Spouse: Isaac
Sons: Esau, Jacob
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Rebekah was the wife of Isaac and the mother of Esau and Jacob.
Verses: Genesis 24:15-67, Genesis 25:20-26

# Bethuel
Parents: Nahor (son of Terah), Milcah
Sons: Laban
Daughters: Rebekah
Summary: Bethuel was the son of Nahor and Milcah and the father of Rebekah and Laban.
Verses: Genesis 22:20-23, Genesis 24:15, Genesis 24:29

# Laban
Parents: Bethuel
Sons: Unknown
Daughters: Leah, Rachel
Summary: Laban was Rebekah’s brother and the father of Leah and Rachel.
Verses: Genesis 24:29-30, Genesis 29:16

# Esau
Parents: Isaac, Rebekah
Spouse: Unknown
Sons: Eliphaz, Reuel, Jeush, Jalam, Korah
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Esau was the older twin brother of Jacob. His descendants included the Edomites.
Verses: Genesis 25:24-26, Genesis 36:1-19

# Jacob
Parents: Isaac, Rebekah
Spouse: Leah, Rachel, Bilhah, Zilpah
Sons: Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Dan, Naphtali, Gad, Asher, Issachar, Zebulun, Joseph, Benjamin
Daughters: Dinah
Summary: Jacob was later renamed Israel. He fathered twelve sons who became the ancestors of the tribes of Israel.
Verses: Genesis 25:26, Genesis 29:31-35, Genesis 30:1-24, Genesis 35:16-18

# Leah
Parents: Laban
Spouse: Jacob
Sons: Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Zebulun
Daughters: Dinah
Summary: Leah was Jacob’s first wife and the mother of six of his sons and his daughter Dinah.
Verses: Genesis 29:16-35, Genesis 30:17-21

# Rachel
Parents: Laban
Spouse: Jacob
Sons: Joseph, Benjamin
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Rachel was Jacob’s beloved wife and the mother of Joseph and Benjamin.
Verses: Genesis 29:6, Genesis 29:28, Genesis 30:22-24, Genesis 35:16-20

# Bilhah
Parents: Unknown
Spouse: Jacob
Sons: Dan, Naphtali
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Bilhah was Rachel’s servant and the mother of Dan and Naphtali by Jacob.
Verses: Genesis 30:3-8

# Zilpah
Parents: Unknown
Spouse: Jacob
Sons: Gad, Asher
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Zilpah was Leah’s servant and the mother of Gad and Asher by Jacob.
Verses: Genesis 30:9-13

# Reuben
Parents: Jacob, Leah
Sons: Unknown
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Reuben was Jacob’s firstborn son.
Verses: Genesis 29:32, Genesis 35:22-23

# Simeon
Parents: Jacob, Leah
Sons: Unknown
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Simeon was the second son of Jacob and Leah.
Verses: Genesis 29:33, Genesis 35:23

# Levi
Parents: Jacob, Leah
Sons: Gershon, Kohath, Merari
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Levi was the third son of Jacob and Leah. His descendants became the Levitical priestly tribe.
Verses: Genesis 29:34, Genesis 46:11

# Judah
Parents: Jacob, Leah
Sons: Er, Onan, Shelah, Perez, Zerah
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Judah was the fourth son of Jacob and Leah. The family line leading to King David and Jesus later runs through Perez.
Verses: Genesis 29:35, Genesis 38:1-30, Genesis 46:12

# Dan
Parents: Jacob, Bilhah
Sons: Unknown
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Dan was the son of Jacob and Bilhah.
Verses: Genesis 30:5-6, Genesis 46:23

# Naphtali
Parents: Jacob, Bilhah
Sons: Unknown
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Naphtali was the son of Jacob and Bilhah.
Verses: Genesis 30:7-8, Genesis 46:24

# Gad
Parents: Jacob, Zilpah
Sons: Unknown
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Gad was the son of Jacob and Zilpah.
Verses: Genesis 30:9-11, Genesis 46:16

# Asher
Parents: Jacob, Zilpah
Sons: Unknown
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Asher was the son of Jacob and Zilpah.
Verses: Genesis 30:12-13, Genesis 46:17

# Issachar
Parents: Jacob, Leah
Sons: Unknown
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Issachar was the son of Jacob and Leah.
Verses: Genesis 30:17-18, Genesis 46:13

# Zebulun
Parents: Jacob, Leah
Sons: Unknown
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Zebulun was the son of Jacob and Leah.
Verses: Genesis 30:19-20, Genesis 46:14

# Dinah
Parents: Jacob, Leah
Sons: Unknown
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Dinah was the daughter of Jacob and Leah.
Verses: Genesis 30:21, Genesis 34:1-31

# Joseph
Parents: Jacob, Rachel
Spouse: Asenath
Sons: Manasseh, Ephraim
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Joseph was sold into Egypt by his brothers and later became a powerful ruler there. His sons were Manasseh and Ephraim.
Verses: Genesis 30:22-24, Genesis 37:1-36, Genesis 41:45-52

# Benjamin
Parents: Jacob, Rachel
Sons: Unknown
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Benjamin was the youngest son of Jacob and Rachel.
Verses: Genesis 35:16-18, Genesis 46:19

# Perez
Parents: Judah, Tamar
Sons: Hezron, Hamul
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Perez was the son of Judah and Tamar. His descendants include the line leading to King David.
Verses: Genesis 38:27-30, Genesis 46:12

# Zerah
Parents: Judah, Tamar
Sons: Unknown
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Zerah was the twin brother of Perez and the son of Judah and Tamar.
Verses: Genesis 38:27-30

# Tamar
Parents: Unknown
Spouse: Judah
Sons: Perez, Zerah
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Tamar was the mother of Judah’s twin sons Perez and Zerah.
Verses: Genesis 38:6-30

# Asenath
Parents: Potiphera
Spouse: Joseph
Sons: Manasseh, Ephraim
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Asenath was the Egyptian wife of Joseph and the mother of Manasseh and Ephraim.
Verses: Genesis 41:45, Genesis 41:50-52

# Manasseh
Parents: Joseph, Asenath
Sons: Unknown
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Manasseh was the elder son of Joseph and Asenath.
Verses: Genesis 41:50-51, Genesis 46:20

# Ephraim
Parents: Joseph, Asenath
Sons: Unknown
Daughters: Unknown
Summary: Ephraim was the younger son of Joseph and Asenath.
Verses: Genesis 41:50-52, Genesis 46:20
